Limits

    Model: A → B → C, both first-order and irreversible, in an isothermal CSTR with feed concentration 2 kmol/m³. Rate constants follow Arrhenius laws (k₂ rises faster with temperature, so hotter means more waste). Profit = B sales − feed cost − C disposal − heating − reactor cost. The optimizer runs Nelder–Mead from many random starts with a penalty for broken limits.
